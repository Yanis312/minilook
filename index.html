Mini Look publie des clips vidéo courts sur ses propres comptes TikTok, YouTube et Instagram.

Mini Look publishes short video clips on its own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. clipping-pipeline is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of Mini Look to schedule and publish their own videos through the official platform APIs.
